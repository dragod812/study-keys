The margin is a thinking space.

An essay for testing reading tools · 5 min read

Reading becomes more useful when we pause long enough to notice what changed our mind. A highlight marks that moment. A note records why it mattered.

The strongest notes are often questions. What assumption made this argument possible? Where else could this pattern apply? What evidence would change the conclusion?

Capture the connection while it is still fresh.

Make the return journey easy

We rarely remember the exact words. We remember a shape of an idea, a surprising example, or the conversation that followed. Searchable notes turn those fragments into a way back.

Color can provide a small vocabulary: an idea, a reusable pattern, a technical detail, an open question, or something to revisit. The vocabulary should be stable enough to become a habit.

Leave room for uncertainty

A note does not have to be a conclusion. Sometimes the useful thing is to preserve a question without resolving it.

Reliable tools should do the same. When a document changes and a passage cannot be located confidently, keep the annotation intact and acknowledge that the passage has moved.
