Detect a queue that is stuck

Check the age of the oldest item before adding capacity. A stalled dependency can leave the queue growing even when many workers are idle.

Stabilize the dependency

Reduce admissions until latency returns to its normal range. Protect a small amount of capacity for health checks and recovery work.

Measure recovery

Track latency, error rate, pending work and throughput. A lower queue depth alone does not prove that requests are completing successfully.

Restore the usual budget

Increase concurrency gradually and compare each step with a baseline. Keep rollback simple enough to perform quickly.
